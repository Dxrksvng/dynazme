## What to expect on a discovery call

- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Cost**: free
- **What we ask**: about your workflow, the time it costs you, what you've tried, what you'd pay for a result that worked
- **What we don't do**: ship a slide deck, push for a close, ask for an NDA before the conversation starts

Three outcomes are possible:

1. **There's a fit** — we send a written pilot proposal within one business day, with scope, timeline, and price.
2. **There's a tool that fits better** — we name it and explain why. No engagement.
3. **The problem is too early or too custom for Dynazme today** — we say so, and where useful, suggest what would have to be true for the conversation to be different.
