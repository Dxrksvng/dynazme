# Vision

> Where Dynazme is going — and why a Thai-first AI engineering boutique needs to exist.

---

## The five-year position

By **mid-2031**, Dynazme is the default first call for a Thai mid-market company that needs production AI engineering and refuses to import a foreign vendor.

Not the biggest. Not the cheapest. The one a CFO trusts not to oversell, an ops director trusts to ship, and a compliance officer trusts to take PDPA seriously.

That position is earned by:

- Doing the work. No reseller margin. No subcontracted offshore team. The founder and a small senior team write the code that goes into production.
- Saying no. To 80% of inbound. To product expansion that doesn't earn its place. To customers whose problem is not a Dynazme-shaped problem.
- Being right about Thailand. Not "AI translated into Thai" — AI designed knowing the language, the institutions, and the regulatory floor.

---

## What changes if Dynazme is right

If the bet works:

- A Thai mid-market firm can adopt production AI without writing a foreign-currency cheque.
- A Thai compliance officer has a vendor who knows what Section 28 of PDPA actually means before the conversation starts.
- A Thai engineer at a customer site has a Thai counterpart on the other side of the integration call, not a Bangalore time-zone-shifted overlap window.
- A Thai founder can point to a category — "AI engineering boutique" — that did not exist as a credible business in 2026.

---

## What stays the same

The vision is not a product roadmap. The four product lines may consolidate, expand, or be replaced by what customer reality demands. What does not change:

- Thai-first, not Thai-translated
- Production-grade, not demo-grade
- Honest disclosure over heroic claim
- Boutique scale — depth in a niche, not breadth in a brochure

---

## What this is not

To keep the vision honest, here is what Dynazme is **not** trying to become:

- A consulting firm that bills by the hour and avoids product
- A SaaS company chasing a US-style ARR multiple
- A frontier-AI lab — model research is not the moat
- A "Thai version of \[Foreign Brand\]" — the position is built on what Thai customers need, not on imitation
- A platform serving 100,000 SMEs through a self-service tier — that's a different business and a different founder

---

## Why this matters now

Three windows are closing:

1. **Foreign vendors are not yet credible in Thai compliance.** They will become credible eventually. Until they do, there is a Thailand-shaped gap that Thai-resident teams are positioned to fill.
2. **AI tooling is briefly cheap to build.** The combination of capable open-source models, mature retrieval libraries, and shrinking GPU costs means the cost-of-entry to build production AI in 2026 is lower than it has been or will be again.
3. **PDPA enforcement is ramping.** PDPC inspections, SEC scrutiny on AI-driven trading, and BOT cloud-outsourcing rules are creating mandatory work that someone has to do. A vendor that takes this seriously today is positioned for the regulated procurement of 2027–2028.

If Dynazme is built right in 2026–2027, the position compounds. If those 18 months are spent on the wrong thing, the windows close and the position is no longer available.

---

## How the vision is tested

The vision is not load-bearing during day-to-day decisions. The roadmap is. ([`ROADMAP.md`](./ROADMAP.md).)

But every decision should pass two checks:

1. **Does this move Dynazme towards the five-year position?** If not, why are we doing it?
2. **Does this make the founder unable to back out?** If yes, slow down.

This document exists to answer the first question. The kill criteria in the roadmap exist to answer the second.
