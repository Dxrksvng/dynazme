# Roadmap

> Honest, dated, revisable. Targets are real intent, not promises. Kill criteria are real — building the wrong thing for too long is a worse outcome than admitting it.
>
> Phase contents are kept high-level here. Detailed deliverables and engagement-level plans live in private planning docs.

---

## North Star

- **Year 5 (mid-2031)** — Dynazme is the default Thai-resident AI engineering boutique for mid-market companies. Recognised, profitable, small senior team, no investor distortion.
- **Year 1 (mid-2027)** — repeatable engagement motion, ≥3 referenceable customers, profitable on a $0 paid-acquisition basis.
- **Month 6 (end of Oct 2026)** — first paying customer through a scoped pilot. The four product lines have been validated by ≥10 substantive discovery conversations.

These are intent. They will move when reality moves. Honesty over optimism.

## Phase 1 — First Paying Customer — GATED

**Goal**: one signed pilot engagement, paid, with a defined success metric, against a customer's real workflow.

**Pre-condition**: ≥5 substantive customer-discovery conversations (≥30 min each, with a buyer or buyer-adjacent decision-maker), with consistent pain surfaced in at least 3 of them. No engineering work begins on Phase 1 deliverables before this gate is met.

Phase 1 covers: a Dynazme-side discovery call; a written pilot proposal; the engineering work to deliver the pilot; weekly check-ins; a measurable success metric; and a post-pilot decision to convert or close cleanly.

**Definition of done**: pilot delivered against the agreed metric, customer either converts to a production engagement or signs a clean close. Either outcome is acceptable; an indefinite pilot is not.

---

## Phase 2 — Three Customers — GATED

**Goal**: ≥3 referenceable customers across at least two of the four product lines.

Phase 2 covers: standardising the engagement playbook; productising the most-repeated pilot shapes; first written case studies (with customer permission); first public talks where they support customer acquisition; first hire if and only if the unit economics support it.

**Definition of done**: three customers running Dynazme work in production for ≥30 days each. At least two are willing to provide a reference call to a future prospect.

---

## Phase 3 — Repeatable Engagement Motion — FUTURE

**Goal**: a sales motion that does not require the founder to be in every first call, and a delivery model that does not require the founder to write every line of code.

Phase 3 covers: senior-engineer hire #1 and #2; documented engagement playbooks; outsourced ops where it doesn't compromise quality; the second compliance pack (sector-specific, e.g. PDPA-and-ก.ล.ต. for finance customers); the first investor conversation if and only if growth requires it.

**Definition of done**: a real recurring-revenue milestone; founder time on first calls below 50%; engagements being delivered by named team members other than the founder.

---

## Phase 4 — Scale — FUTURE

**Goal**: a real team and a real revenue run rate, without losing the boutique posture.

Phase 4 covers: small senior team; vertical packs for the highest-regulation industries; partnerships where they accelerate the customer's compliance posture; SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / ISO 42001 attestation work; structured pricing tiers replacing per-engagement pricing for the most repeatable shapes.

## Kill criteria

We stop or pivot if any of these is true at the named milestone. Killing the wrong project early frees the founder for the right one.

| When | Kill if |
|---|---|
| End of customer-discovery window (≤90 days) | Fewer than 3 of the discovery conversations surfaced the same specific pain |
| End of Phase 1 | No customer signed a pilot, or pilot ran without conversion conversation |
| After 3 pilots | Zero pilots converted to a production engagement |
| End of Phase 2 | Fewer than 3 customers running Dynazme work in production |
| End of Phase 3 | Recurring revenue meaningfully below target AND no clear path to it within the next two quarters |
| Anytime | A foreign incumbent (Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, AWS) ships a Thai-localised AI product at price parity AND distribution that cannot be matched within available capital |
| Anytime | Founder personal runway falls below 6 months AND no signed engagement covers the next milestone — the choice is then to take contract work, raise, or stop. "Quietly continue" is not an option |

These are real — written down on purpose so the founder cannot quietly move the goalposts later. The runway-bound row exists specifically because self-funded solo founders are the population most prone to indefinite drift.

## What this roadmap is not

- A product roadmap. Product decisions follow customer reality, not this document.
- A commitment to investors or customers. There are none of either.
- A guarantee of any phase landing on schedule. Reality moves; the gates are what stay fixed.
