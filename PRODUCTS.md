# Products

Dynazme is developing four product lines. None has a paying customer yet. All are in active engineering — the reference implementations live in private; what you see below is the product-level position.

Pricing is intentionally not published. Setup and monthly run rates are agreed after a discovery call, calibrated to the customer's existing software spend and the time-cost of the workflow being replaced.

---

## 1. FahMai — Thai-First RAG

> Search and Q&A over Thai documents. Built for the businesses where ChatGPT can't see the policy manual.

**Problem**

- Customer-facing teams answer the same Thai-language questions dozens of times a day.
- Internal staff cannot find the SOP, policy, or warranty document buried in a SharePoint nobody indexes.
- New hires take 2–3 months to internalise institutional knowledge that already exists, written down.
- Foreign LLMs (GPT-class, Claude) are excellent in English; on Thai legal, medical, or technical Thai, they are inconsistent.

**Approach**

Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + dense + RRF) tuned for Thai, with grounded answers, source-document citations, and a refusal mode when retrieval confidence is below threshold.

**Buyers**

Customer support, HR, legal, and operations teams in companies with >100 internal documents and a Thai-first user base.

**Stage**

Reference implementation built (Super AI Hackathon ss6). Core retrieval logic re-engineered in private. Customer-facing front-end on hold pending first design partner.

## 2. BI Agent — Data → Executive Presentation

> CSV in, board-ready slide deck out. The monthly report your analyst spent two days on, in twenty minutes.

**Problem**

- Analysts spend 1–3 days per cycle assembling slides for executive review.
- Slides look polished but bury the insight under chart-junk.
- Marketing rebuilds the same deck every quarter from scratch.
- Executives ask "so what?" and the analyst answers in another deck a week later.

**Approach**

Statistical pre-analysis (numbers computed in code, not generated by an LLM), narrative-first slide planning, design system enforcement, and a verifier pass that flags any number on a slide that doesn't trace back to the source data.

**Buyers**

Analyst teams, consulting firms, marketing departments, and finance teams that produce recurring reports for executive audiences.

**Stage**

v2 prototype built (consulting pipeline, executive-agent narrative layer, verifier). Re-engineering for production reliability in private.

## 3. DocuSense — Thai Document OCR

> Pull structured data out of Thai paper. Receipts, tax invoices, application forms, exam papers, hand-written notes.

**Problem**

- Accounting teams key receipts into Excel by hand — slow, error-prone, and the original is destroyed.
- HR reads hundreds of resumes manually and still misses the right candidate.
- Legacy archives sit in scanned form, unsearchable, until someone funds a digitisation project that never happens.
- Generic OCR engines mis-read Thai, especially handwriting and rotated documents.

**Approach**

Document-aware preprocessing (rotation correction, perspective unwarping, contrast normalisation), Thai-tuned OCR, schema-driven extraction, and human-in-the-loop review for low-confidence fields.

**Buyers**

Accounting, HR, legal, and back-office teams with paper-heavy workflows. Adjacent fits: insurance claims, university admissions, healthcare records.

**Stage**

Senior-project foundation (KMITL English Exam OCR). Productionising the preprocessing and extraction pipeline in private.

## 4. FlowForge — AI Workflow Automation

> The AI that lives inside your operations, not on your screen. LINE OA, email, Sheets, ERP — one flow.

**Problem**

- AI tools rarely talk to the systems a Thai SME actually uses (LINE OA, Google Sheets, PEAK, FlowAccount, Make.com).
- "AI chatbot" projects deliver a chatbot, then leave. The chat does not update the CRM, file the ticket, or trigger the quote.
- End-to-end automation requires stitching n8n, Zapier, and a handful of Python scripts — fragile and hard to hand off.

**Approach**

A single workflow runtime that composes FahMai, BI Agent, and DocuSense as building blocks, with first-class connectors to LINE OA, Email, Google Sheets, common Thai SME accounting platforms, and an extension surface for customer-specific systems.

**Buyers**

SMEs and mid-market firms that already have a clear, repetitive, multi-system workflow ready to be automated. We don't sell to companies whose workflow is itself unclear.

**Stage**

Skeleton scaffolded. Awaiting the first customer-driven scope before further engineering — building it without a real workflow risks producing a generic n8n re-skin nobody needs.

## How engagements work

1. **Discovery call (free, 30 min)** — we ask, you describe. No slide deck, no pitch.
2. **Scoped pilot** — if there is a fit, we propose a 4–8 week pilot bound by a specific success metric.
3. **Production engagement** — pilots that hit the metric convert to a production engagement with an agreed monthly run rate.
4. **Walk away clauses** — if the pilot does not hit the metric, the engagement closes and the customer pays only the agreed pilot fee.

This isn't generosity. It's how Dynazme avoids building products nobody actually uses.

---

## What Dynazme will not build

To stay disciplined, the following are out of scope:

- Generic "AI chatbot" projects with no defined workflow outcome
- Marketing-content generation tools (the field is saturated and undifferentiated)
- Image / video generation products (not a Thai-business pain point at the SME tier)
- Trading bots, signal generators, or anything that auto-executes financial transactions
- Anything that requires us to misrepresent AI capability to close a deal

If the work doesn't fit the four product lines, we say so — and where useful, point to a tool or vendor that does fit.
