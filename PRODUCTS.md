# Products

Four product lines, all in active development. Source code is private.

---

## FahMai — Thai-First RAG

Search and Q&A across Thai documents.
For teams that answer the same questions about company policies, products, or processes again and again.

## BI Agent — Data → Slide

Turn business data into executive-ready presentations.
For analysts, finance, and marketing teams that produce recurring reports.

## DocuSense — Thai Document OCR

Extract structured data from Thai receipts, forms, and invoices.
For accounting, HR, and back-office teams whose work is gated by paper.

## FlowForge — AI Workflow Automation

End-to-end automation across LINE OA, Email, Sheets, and existing systems.
For SMEs whose AI tools currently don't talk to each other.
